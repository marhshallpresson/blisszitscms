﻿﻿# Blisszits — Business Executive Document
## A Mobile-First Social Media Management Platform

## 1. Executive Summary
Blisszits is a mobile-first social media management platform designed for creators, startups, and small businesses that need powerful social media tools without the complexity and cost of enterprise platforms. It brings planning, approvals, publishing readiness, inbox triage, and performance views into a single workspace so teams can run content operations without tool sprawl. The current product is a Firebase-based MVP with clearly labeled simulated areas for inbox and analytics data sources.

## 2. Market Opportunity
Businesses increasingly rely on social media as a primary growth channel. Most teams manage multiple platforms and need a reliable way to plan, publish, and measure content. The market is crowded with enterprise tools that are priced and built for large teams, leaving a gap for affordable, simple, and mobile-first workflows.

Typical platform coverage includes:
- Instagram
- Facebook
- TikTok
- LinkedIn
- YouTube
- X (Twitter)
- Pinterest

The market has three common pain points:
1. Pricing barriers driven by per-user or per-seat pricing.
2. Complexity from enterprise-heavy features that small teams do not need.
3. Fragmented workflows across multiple tools.

## 3. Product Overview
Blisszits delivers a unified social media management stack in one platform.

### Core Product Capabilities
1. Content Planning Workspace
   - Content calendar, drafts, and campaign planning.
   - Team collaboration with clear status visibility.
2. AI Content Assistant
   - Caption writing, hashtag suggestions, content ideas, and repurposing.
3. Multi-Platform Scheduling
   - Scheduling with bulk uploads and optimal posting time guidance.
4. Unified Social Inbox
   - Centralized comments, direct messages, mentions, and replies.
5. Performance Analytics
   - Engagement tracking, audience growth, and content performance views.

## 10. Scope Definition
### In Scope Today
- Multi-workspace content planning and lifecycle management.
- AI-assisted content drafting (plans, captions, scripts, hashtags).
- Approval and scheduling readiness workflows.
- Workspace-based inbox and analytics experiences with seeded data.
- Social account organization with metadata-based connection records.
- Server-managed scheduling and retry workflows.

### Out of Scope Today
- Live, multi-network inbox ingestion from social platforms.
- Live analytics connectors for real performance data.
- Full production publishing across all networks.

## 11. User Personas
1. Creator-Operator
   - Runs a small content operation and needs a clear weekly workflow.
   - Values fast ideation and simple approvals.
2. UGC Strategist
   - Manages scripts and drafts for multiple client workspaces.
   - Needs predictable lifecycle stages and review status visibility.
3. Early-Stage Agency Lead
   - Coordinates client approvals, reporting, and multi-workspace work.
   - Needs consistent processes and scalable collaboration.

## 12. Core User Workflows
1. Onboard and set up a workspace.
2. Plan and draft content with AI assistance.
3. Review and approve content using lifecycle stages.
4. Schedule and monitor publishing readiness.
5. Triage and respond to engagement.
6. Review performance snapshots to inform planning.

## 13. Integration Requirements
- Social networks are represented as capabilities and connection records today.
- OAuth and live network connectivity are partially implemented for select networks.
- Webhook entry points exist for future inbox ingestion.
- Internal sync endpoints support seeded inbox and analytics data.

## 14. Security and Compliance Considerations
- Workspace ownership and access are enforced at the data level.
- Sensitive operations are executed through authenticated server routes.
- Internal automation routes require a secret key.
- Audit logs capture sensitive workspace operations.

## 15. Maintenance Procedures
- Verify health endpoints during deployment and ongoing monitoring.
- Use standard quality gates for release checks.
- Rotate secret keys and OAuth credentials regularly.
- Review audit logs and access patterns for unusual activity.

## 16. Success Measures (Business-Level)
- Reduced content planning time per week.
- Higher on-time publishing rates.
- Increased team throughput per workspace.
- Lower tool overlap and subscription costs.
- Stronger stakeholder trust due to clear capability labeling.
